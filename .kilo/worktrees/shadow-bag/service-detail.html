Princess Cake FAQ

Common Questions

How many tiers can the Princess cake have?
+
We can create princess cakes with 1 to 5 tiers. The price and lead time increases with each tier. Our most popular choice is the 3-tier Classic which serves 35–55 guests beautifully.
Can I choose specific character princesses?
+
While we cannot use copyrighted licensed characters directly, we create stunning princess-inspired designs based on character themes — castle environments, signature colour palettes, iconic accessories. Contact us with your child's favourite and we'll design something magical.
Is the crown topper edible or a keepsake?
+
We offer both options! Edible fondant crowns are included in the base price. For an additional $15, we can also create a non-edible acrylic or metal crown topper that your child can keep as a birthday memento.
How should I store and serve the cake?
+
Our cakes are best at room temperature. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. If your environment is warm (over 25°C), refrigerate and bring back to room temp 2 hours before serving for best flavour and texture.
