Common Questions

Quick Answers

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?
+
We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on business days. During busy periods (school holidays, December), it may take up to 48 hours. For urgent requests, please call us directly.
Can I visit the studio without an appointment?
+
Our studio is a working kitchen, so we ask all visitors to book a consultation appointment in advance. This ensures we have dedicated time to discuss your cake requirements properly. Appointments typically last 30–45 minutes.
Do you offer urgent/rush cake orders?
+
Rush orders (within 7 days) may be possible depending on our current schedule and cake complexity. Rush orders incur a 25% surcharge. Please call us directly to discuss and check availability — don't hesitate even if the date is close!
What information do you need to provide a quote?
+
To provide an accurate quote, it helps to know: your party date, estimated guest count (for sizing), preferred theme/design, any dietary requirements, and your location (for delivery pricing). Inspiration photos are always welcome!
Can I change my order after placing it?
+
Minor changes (flavours, name spelling, colour tweaks) can usually be accommodated up to 2 weeks before delivery. Major design changes may incur additional costs and are subject to availability. Please contact us as early as possible if anything changes.
