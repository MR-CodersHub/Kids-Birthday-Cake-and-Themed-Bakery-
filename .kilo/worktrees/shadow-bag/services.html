Service FAQ

Service Questions

What's included in the cake price?
+
All cakes include: fresh premium sponge, fillings, exterior fondant/buttercream, themed decorations, name/age topper and basic fondant figurine. Premium add-ons are priced separately.
How long does a cake last?
+
Our cakes are best enjoyed within 3–4 days of collection/delivery. Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Refrigerate if your home is warm — bring back to room temp before serving.
Can I taste test before ordering?
+
Yes! We offer tasting boxes of 6 flavour samples for £25 (redeemable against your order). Contact us to arrange a tasting appointment at our studio.
Do you provide setup at the venue?
+
Setup and display services are available for Grand and Epic tier cakes, and as an add-on for other sizes. Our team can set up cake tables, arrange flowers and position the cake perfectly.
