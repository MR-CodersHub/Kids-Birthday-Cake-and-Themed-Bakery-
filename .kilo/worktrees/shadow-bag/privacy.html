1. Overview

Sugar Bliss Bakery Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website at sugarbliss.co.uk or use our services.

We process your data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and other applicable privacy laws. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data Controller: Sugar Bliss Bakery Ltd, 42 Sweet Lane, London W1B 3AG, UK
Contact: privacy@sugarbliss.co.uk

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

Information You Provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number and delivery address when placing orders
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Birthday details for reminder services (provided voluntarily)
  • Communications you send us via email, contact forms or social media
  • Account credentials when you register on our website

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Browser type, IP address, pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Device information and screen resolution
  • Referral source and click patterns via analytics tools
  • Cookie data (see Section 5 for details)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your cake orders
  • Sending order confirmations, delivery updates and receipts
  • Providing customer service and responding to enquiries
  • Sending birthday reminders (only if you opt in)
  • Marketing communications (only with your explicit consent)
  • Improving our website and services through analytics
  • Complying with legal obligations and fraud prevention

We only process your data where we have a lawful basis to do so, including your consent, contract performance, legal obligation, or our legitimate interests.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Delivery partners: To fulfil your cake delivery (name, address and contact only)
  • Payment processors: Stripe processes payments securely on our behalf
  • Email services: Mailchimp helps us send order confirmations and newsletters
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymised) helps us improve our website
  • Legal authorities: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

All third-party partners are contractually required to protect your data and may only use it as directed by us.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our website. Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (shopping cart, session management)
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings like dark mode and language
  • Marketing cookies: Only with consent, for personalised advertising

You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie banner or your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive account information
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Strict staff access controls and data handling training
  • We retain order data for 7 years as required by UK tax law, then securely delete it

Despite our security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing: Request we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@sugarbliss.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for adults ordering cakes for children's celebrations. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13. If a parent provides a child's name or birthday for our reminder service, this information is used solely for that purpose and is never shared with third parties or used for marketing.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
