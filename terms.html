2. Ordering Process

All orders are subject to acceptance by Sugar Bliss Bakery. An order is only confirmed when you receive a written confirmation email from us and have paid the required deposit.

  • Orders must be placed at least 14 days before the required delivery date (minimum 4 weeks for complex 4+ tier designs)
  • Rush orders (under 7 days notice) are subject to availability and incur a 25% surcharge
  • Design specifications, flavours and personalisation must be confirmed in writing
  • We reserve the right to refuse any order that we are unable to fulfil to our quality standards
  • Significant design changes after order confirmation may require a revised quote
  • We cannot guarantee exact colour matching as monitor displays vary

We will provide a design sketch for approval before commencing work on bespoke orders. Your written approval constitutes agreement with the final design.

3. Payment Terms

  • Deposit: A non-refundable 50% deposit is required to confirm and reserve your booking date
  • Balance: The remaining balance is due 7 days before the agreed delivery date
  • Late payment: Orders where balance is not received within 7 days before delivery may be cancelled at our discretion
  • Payment methods: Bank transfer, major credit/debit cards (via Stripe), PayPal
  • Pricing: All prices are in GBP and include VAT at the prevailing rate
  • Price changes: Confirmed order prices are fixed. We reserve the right to change standard pricing without notice

Payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store, process or have access to your full card details.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that circumstances can change. Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • More than 4 weeks before delivery: Full deposit refund minus a £25 administration fee
  • 2–4 weeks before delivery: 50% of deposit refunded. Design costs may be deducted if work has commenced
  • Less than 2 weeks before delivery: No deposit refund. Full balance may be charged if materials have been ordered
  • If we cancel: Full refund of all payments made if we are unable to fulfil your order

We strongly recommend purchasing event or wedding insurance to cover any unexpected cancellations.

Cancellations must be made in writing via email to orders@sugarbliss.co.uk and are effective from the date we acknowledge receipt.

5. Delivery Terms

  • Delivery is available within Greater London (up to 10km free with Royale/Diamond packages, otherwise charged)
  • Delivery times are agreed at order confirmation and are estimated, not guaranteed
  • Someone must be available to receive the delivery — failed delivery attempts may incur redelivery charges
  • We are not responsible for damage that occurs after the cake has been signed for and accepted
  • Extreme weather or unforeseen circumstances may affect delivery — we will notify you as early as possible
  • Collection from our studio is available by prior arrangement

All cakes are transported in climate-controlled conditions and specially designed protective packaging. Upon delivery, please inspect your cake and report any issues immediately.

6. Quality Guarantee

We take immense pride in the quality of our cakes. If you are not satisfied with your order:

  • Report any issues within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence

  • Complaints about taste or flavour must be reported within 12 hours of delivery

Please note: our cakes are handmade artisan products. Slight variations from design sketches or reference images are a natural characteristic of artisan craftsmanship and are not grounds for complaint.
