Pricing FAQ

Pricing Questions

Are the prices shown final?
+
The prices shown are starting prices. Your final quote depends on the complexity of your design, number of tiers, special ingredients and any add-ons. We always provide a detailed, itemised quote before you commit — no surprises!
When do I pay?
+
We require a 50% deposit to confirm your booking and date. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your delivery date. We accept bank transfer, credit/debit card and PayPal.
Do you offer payment plans?
+
For Diamond tier orders over $400, we offer a 3-month interest-free payment plan. Contact us for details — we want every family to be able to access our magic!
What is your cancellation policy?
+
Cancellations more than 4 weeks before delivery: full deposit refund. 2–4 weeks: 50% deposit refund. Less than 2 weeks: no refund (materials already purchased). We recommend event insurance for added peace of mind.
