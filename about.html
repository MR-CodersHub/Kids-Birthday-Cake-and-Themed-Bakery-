Our Story

The Sweet Story Behind
Sugar Bliss Bakery

From a home kitchen to a luxury cake studio — discover the passion, people and craft behind every magical celebration.

Founded 2018

Born from a Mom's Love & a Passion for Art

It all started in a small home kitchen in South London when Clara Whitmore, a trained pastry chef and mother of two, couldn't find a birthday cake that truly matched her daughter's love for unicorns.

So she made one herself. And then another. And another. Her kitchen became a magical studio, word spread, and Sugar Bliss Bakery was born — a boutique where edible art meets childhood dreams.

Today, we serve hundreds of families across London and beyond, crafting luxury children's cakes that don't just look extraordinary — they taste like pure joy.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every cake we bake, every candle we place, every smile we create.

Passion in Every Layer

We pour genuine love into every cake. No shortcuts, no compromises — just artistry, care and dedication to perfection.

Pure & Premium

Organic flour, free-range eggs, natural colorants, real butter. What goes into your child's cake matters — so we only use the finest.

Artistry Without Limits

We treat each cake as a canvas. Our designs push boundaries of what edible art can be — whimsical, detailed, and uniquely yours.

Families First

Every interaction is personal. We listen, we collaborate, we care. Your family's joy is our greatest achievement.

Sustainable Baking

Eco-friendly packaging, local sourcing, zero food waste policies. We bake with the planet in mind — for the children who'll inherit it.

Moment Makers

Birthdays are precious — we understand the weight of that magic moment. We don't make cakes; we make memories that last forever.

Meet the Artists

The Talented Hands Behind the Magic

Three extraordinary artisans, united by a love of childhood wonder and the craft of sugar.

Founder & Head Pastry Artist
Clara Whitmore

Award-winning pastry chef trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris. Clara founded Sugar Bliss to give children the extraordinary birthday cakes they deserve. Her princess designs are legendary.

Lead Sugar Artist & Designer
Sarah Chen

Former fine arts graduate who discovered her passion for edible sculpture. Sarah specialises in hyper-realistic fondant figurines and character cake toppers that amaze everyone.

Floral Sugar Specialist
Maria Santos

Botanically-trained sugar flower artist with a gift for creating breathtaking edible floral arrangements. Every petal Maria crafts could be mistaken for the real thing.

Our Journey

8 Years of Growing Magic

From a home kitchen to London's most celebrated children's cake studio — here's how Sugar Bliss became a fairytale in itself.

2018
The Spark

Clara bakes her first custom unicorn cake for her daughter's 5th birthday — and receives 30 orders from party guests the next day.

2019
First Studio

Sugar Bliss opens its first boutique kitchen studio in Notting Hill. Sarah joins as lead designer. First month: 45 orders.

2021
Award Recognition

Wins "Best Children's Cake Studio — London" at the Great British Cake Awards. Features in Vogue and Hello magazine.

2023
Expansion & Maria Joins

New flagship studio opens in Covent Garden. Maria Santos joins as floral specialist. Capacity doubles to 60 cakes/week.

2025
Today — More Magic Than Ever

2,500+ families served. 50+ premium themes. Nationwide delivery. A waitlist. And the same love that started it all.
