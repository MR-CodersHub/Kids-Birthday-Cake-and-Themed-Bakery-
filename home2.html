Quick Answers

Common Questions

How far in advance should I order?
+
We recommend ordering at least 2–3 weeks in advance for standard cakes. For complex multi-tier designs, 4–6 weeks is ideal. Rush orders (7 days) are sometimes possible — contact us to check availability.
Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?
+
Absolutely! We offer gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free and fully vegan cake options. All dietary requirements are handled with the same premium ingredients and care. Please specify when ordering.
Can I bring my own design or idea?
+
We love unique ideas! Share photos, sketches, or even just a mood board and our team will bring your vision to life. Completely custom designs are our specialty and we offer a design consultation call to get every detail perfect.
What areas do you deliver to?
+
We deliver across Greater London with free delivery within 10km. For orders outside London, we offer nationwide collection-ready packaging and partnered courier service. Contact us for exact pricing.
